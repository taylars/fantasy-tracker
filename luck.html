Reading the season…

Luck runs from −50 to +50, and 0 is average for this league — luck is zero-sum, so the only fair comparison is the other teams in it. ±50 is two standard deviations, so the ends are real extremes.

It is the average of two things. Record luck is wins against the all-play record — what you'd have if you played the whole league every week — which catches scoring 140 and losing. Schedule luck is what the opponents you drew scored against what the league scored that week, which catches facing the one manager who went off.

Exp is the place the all-play record earns, with that record under it. Swing is how many places the schedule has moved you from it.
